# Map Studio shell audit — status, host states, draw, area, history, Full map, help, dialogs, diagnostics

Scope: Task A4b of the 0.5 UX audit. Evidence: synthetic captures under `tests/browser/.generated/ux-audit/`
(viewed at 320–1440 light/LTR, 390 + 1024 dark, RTL, forced colors) and `frontend/map-studio-v4/` source on `origin/main`.
Severity rules and codes: `docs/ux-audit-0.5.md`. Owner decisions respected: refine `ms-*`; one canvas, one
status strip, one primary action; Stop never asks for confirmation.

## Verdicts

- **Status strip & host states** — State is legible at every width, but consequence is not: "Cleaning · Cleaning in progress"
  says nothing, Stop gives no acknowledgement, failures are silent on home, and host states repeat one message four times.
- **Draw** — Labelled 44 px tools, Undo/Redo and an explicit Move map are solid; Cancel is implicit, the panel is a manual,
  and two drawing models (Zone vs Paint) share three names.
- **Area review** — Form is clean and Delete is a dialog, but re-confirm never says why the map changed or what Confirm vs
  Edit outline will do, and the map does not frame the outline being reviewed.
- **History** — Read-only, pose-free and floor-scoped as required; it is not dated where the eye lands, the canvas looks
  identical to Live, and three controls compete to "return".
- **Full map** — Reversible and keeps Stop (code-verified), but the only expand-looking glyph on screen is Fit; Full map hides behind a
  sidebar icon labelled "Hide cleaning panel" with no visible on-state.
- **Help** — Two inconsistent surfaces (desktop popover without Touch, phone modal with Touch), unreachable in every phone
  workflow and in Draw; the need for it at all signals an inconsistent gesture model.
- **Dialogs** — Focus lands on the safe choice, Tab is trapped, Escape and launcher restore work; delete dialogs do not
  name the object, and `confirmStop`/`error` are dead copy whose sentences the live paths actually need.
- **Diagnostics** — Clearly secondary and privacy-explicit, but shows raw enums, never says who the summary is for, and
  disables Copy exactly when a command is stuck.
